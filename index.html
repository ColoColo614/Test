Mini Plumber Run vrs 1.9.9

Beat 5 longer levels, choose your mode + character, and survive denser antagonists in later levels.

Start: choose mode (1/2/3), then character (1/2/3/4), or click cards. Controls: ← → move, Space / ↑ jump, ↓ enter/drop through, R pause/restart, M change mode, C change character.
